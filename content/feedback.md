## Send your feedback to Matt Chapman

Welcome to my feedback page. Feel free to send me a comment about this site,
or ask a question, or anything else. Screenshots of the featured window
managers and desktops are also welcome, along with configuration files where
applicable.

You can contact me via email as <matt@xwinman.org>. This link will bring up a
compose window if your browser supports it, and is properly configured for
email. Alternatively simply use the address in your favourite email program.
Thank you.
