Twm (Tab Window Manager, or sometimes Tom's Window Manager, after the
principal author Tom LaStrange) provides titlebars, shaped windows, several
forms of icon management, user-defined macro functions, click-to-type and
pointer-driven keyboard focus, and user-specified key and pointer button
bindings.

It has however been left behind by more recent window managers, making it
something of mainly historical interest. It was for some time the only real
choice of window manager, after taking over from the very simplistic uwm.
Nearly every window manager since has borrowed heavily from it, either
directly, or indirectly through other window managers, such as fvwm.
